Concept · v1

Cadence

An AI coach that writes tomorrow's session from how today actually went. Built on Nocturne — dark ground, accent as line and glow, outlined actions.

Assumptions I made
Runner: intermediate, chasing a half-marathon PB on a 14-week block.
The AI's headline job is adaptation — it rewrites the week from recovery signals, and always shows its reasoning. No black box.
Wearable data (HRV, sleep) is already syncing, so onboarding is out of scope here.
Coach voice: warm but specific. Numbers, then a sentence a human would say.
